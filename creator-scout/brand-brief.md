# Ari & Co. — brand brief for creator scouting

Taken from ariandco.uk (October 2026). The scout agent reads this before judging any creator.

## Who we are

- Family-owned Korean skincare from East Grinstead, Sussex. The family has made supplements for 60 years.
- Tagline: **"Skincare, simplified. Korean Skincare. British Simplicity."**
- Four products, one ritual: **cleanse, treat, moisturise, restore.**
  1. Puredew Gel Cleanser (£18): gentle, sulfate-free, hinoki water and centella
  2. Daily C Serum (£26): 15% pure vitamin C, vitamin E, ferulic acid, niacinamide
  3. **Peptide Snail Cream (£26): our hero.** 30% snail filtrate, hyaluronic acid, peptides, B5
  4. Radiance Repair Cream (£22): gentle retinol plus bakuchiol, overnight
  - Complete ritual: £73.60 (worth £92)
- Instagram: [@aricoskincare](https://instagram.com/aricoskincare)

## The vibe

- **Simple.** Against 10-step routines and a new must-have every month. "What your skin needs. Nothing it doesn't."
- **Honest and evidence-led.** The journal explains concentrations and why ingredients were chosen. No hype, nothing "added for show".
- **Warm, family, British.** Calm and understated, not loud or glossy.
- **K-beauty quality, minimal routine.** Made in Korea, thought up in Sussex.

## The creators who fit

**Good fit**
- UK-based (bonus: Sussex, Brighton, Surrey, Kent, London and the South East).
- Skincare, "skinimalism", minimal or capsule routines, K-beauty, ingredient explainers, slow living, calm "get ready with me" content.
- Talks about real skin: dehydration, barrier repair, first retinol, vitamin C, skin in your 30s, 40s and 50s.
- Honest reviewer who says when something doesn't work. Their audience trusts them.
- Calm, natural-light, uncluttered look. Neutral, soft tones.
- Small but engaged: the comments are real conversations, not emoji spam.

**Poor fit / avoid**
- Haul or "shelfie" accounts that push 10+ new products a week (goes against "simplified").
- Mostly paid ads, giveaway loops, or follow-for-follow growth.
- Strict **vegan-only** creators: the hero product contains snail filtrate, so it won't sit right with them or their audience.
- Accounts mostly outside the UK (we only ship in the UK for now).
- Shock or "dermatologist-bashing" content, or medical claims.
- Under-18 creators. Don't contact them.

## Budget and gifting

- Gifted only, no fees. That means **nano (1k–10k)** and **small micro (10k–30k)** creators.
- What to send:
  - Nano tier: Peptide Snail Cream (£26) or a 2-product set.
  - Micro tier, or a very strong fit: the complete ritual (£92 RRP).
- No obligation to post. If they do post, ask them to tag @aricoskincare. They can also get a personal 15% code for their followers (in line with WELCOME15).
