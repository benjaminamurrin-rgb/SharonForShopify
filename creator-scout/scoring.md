# Creator scoring rubric (out of 100)

Score every creator the same way so the shortlist can be compared fairly.

## Hard filters (fail any one and the creator is out)

- Followers between **1,000 and 30,000**
- **UK-based**: says so in their bio, shows UK locations, or most of their audience is clearly British
- Posted in the **last 30 days**
- Not a brand account, a reseller, or under 18
- Not strictly vegan-only (our hero product contains snail filtrate)

## Scores

| Area | Points | How to judge |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement rate | 30 | (average likes + comments over the last 6–12 posts) ÷ followers. ≥6% = 30, 4–6% = 24, 3–4% = 18, 2–3% = 10, under 2% = 0 |
| Brand vibe fit | 25 | Simple routines, calm look, honest tone, skincare-led. Use brand-brief.md |
| Topic fit | 15 | Skincare or K-beauty is most of their content (15). Lifestyle with some skincare (8). Rarely skincare (0) |
| Audience quality | 15 | Real comments that ask questions, a UK audience, no bot or giveaway patterns |
| Gifting openness | 10 | Already does gifted posts, has PR or email in bio, says "open to collabs" |
| Local bonus | 5 | Sussex, Brighton, Surrey, Kent or the South East |

**Penalties**
- -10: more than 1 in 3 of their recent posts are #ad
- -10: they posted about a direct competitor in the last 4 weeks
- -15: signs of bought followers (a sudden jump in followers, generic comments)

## Tiers

- **80+**: Priority. Offer the complete ritual.
- **65–79**: Strong. Offer Peptide Snail Cream or a 2-product set.
- **50–64**: Hold. Keep on the list for later.
- **Under 50**: Skip.
