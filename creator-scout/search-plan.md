# Where to look

Instagram search in Agent-Reach finds **users, not posts**. So mix username searches with the web search and seed-account tricks below.

## 1. Instagram user searches (`agent-reach instagram search "<query>" --limit 20`)

- uk skincare
- skincare uk
- skinimalism
- kbeauty uk
- korean skincare uk
- skincare over 30 uk / over 40 / over 50
- minimal skincare
- skincare honest review uk
- brighton skincare, sussex beauty, london skincare
- esthetician uk (only creators, not clinics)
- slow living uk, mum of two skincare, capsule beauty

## 2. Web search to find handles (`agent-reach web search "<query>"`)

- `site:instagram.com "UK" skincare "gifted" "PR"`
- `site:instagram.com skinimalism UK`
- `site:instagram.com "k-beauty" "UK based"`
- `site:instagram.com "Sussex" OR "Brighton" skincare`
- `"UK micro influencer" skincare list 2026`
- `site:tiktok.com "@" uk skincare minimal routine` (many creators post on both)

Pull out the @handles, then check each one with `agent-reach instagram profile <handle>`.

## 3. Seed snowballing

Small creators tag each other and comment on each other's posts. Once you have 3–5 creators who score 80+:
- Read their recent posts and note other small UK creators who comment often or are tagged.
- Look at who they tag in gifted posts from similar indie UK brands. Those creators already say yes to gifting.

## 4. Other platforms (optional)

- `agent-reach youtube search "uk minimal skincare routine"`: small UK YouTubers often have Instagram accounts too.
- `agent-reach reddit search "skincare uk instagram recommend"` (needs a Reddit login).
