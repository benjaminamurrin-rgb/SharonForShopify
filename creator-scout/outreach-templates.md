# Outreach templates

The agent **only drafts** these. A person at Ari & Co. reviews each one, makes it personal, and sends it. (Agent-Reach is read-only and can't send anything.)

Always fill in `{specific}`: one real detail from a recent post. Without it, it's spam.

## Instagram DM (short)

> Hi {first_name}! I'm {your_name} from Ari & Co., a small family skincare brand in Sussex. I loved your {specific}. It's exactly our idea of skincare: simple and honest.
>
> We make a 4-step Korean ritual (our hero is a 30% snail peptide cream), and we'd love to send you {gift} to try. No strings attached: post only if you genuinely like it. Could I take an address?

> Hi {first_name}, just checking you saw my message about sending you some Ari & Co. No worries at all if it's not for you!

## UK advertising rules (ASA/CMA)

- If it's a **pure gift with no conditions**, creators should still say it was gifted (e.g. "#gifted" or "PR product").
- If **we ask them to post, or control what they say**, the post counts as an **ad**, and they must label it clearly with **"#ad"** at the start.
- Never ask a creator to hide that a product was gifted.
